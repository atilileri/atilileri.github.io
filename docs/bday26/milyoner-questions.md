# Milyoner quiz — question pool

The 60 questions of the Milyoner quiz, decided in
[Grilling: what goes into the Milyoner quiz, and who writes it?](https://github.com/atilileri/atilileri.github.io/issues/185).

## How a Round draws from the pool

- **Bands**: questions 1–6 are easy (Kolay), 7–10 medium (Orta), 11–12 hard (Zor).
- **Topic mix**: the easy band holds each of the five topics once plus one random topic a second
  time; the medium band holds four different topics; the hard band holds two different topics.
- **Draw**: random every Round, nothing tracked. The four choices are shuffled.
- **Audience joker** ("Seyirciye sor"): the right answer gets the largest bar on questions 1–10;
  on 11–12 it does only ~70% of the time.
- **Pool**: one full ladder per topic — 6 easy, 4 medium, 2 hard — so 12 per topic, 60 in total.

In each row the **first choice is the right answer**; the other three are wrong. The site shuffles
them. "Hard" means a keen hobbyist knows it, never specialist trivia.
