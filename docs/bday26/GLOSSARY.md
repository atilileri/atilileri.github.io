# Birthday games

A hidden games site at `/20261209/`, a birthday gift for Çağla. She plays short games, earns
Points, and spends them on Gifts. All text she sees is in Turkish. Charted by a wayfinder map
labelled `wayfinder:map`.

## Language

**Game** (_Oyun_):
One playable activity on the site. A Game gives a score from 0% to 100% on each play.
_Avoid_: Level, challenge

**Best score** (_Rekor_):
The highest score she ever reached in one Game. Only the Best score counts; other plays leave
no trace in her Points.
_Avoid_: High score, total score

**Points** (_Puan_):
The currency of the site. She earns Points from her Best scores and spends them on Gifts.
_Avoid_: Credits, coins, score (a score belongs to one play)

**Balance** (_Bakiye_):
The Points she can spend now: Points earned, minus the price of each Gift she holds.
_Avoid_: Wallet, total

**Gift** (_Hediye_):
A present she buys with Points. Its price leaves her Balance at once.
_Avoid_: Reward, unlock, prize (a prize is not bought)

**Small prize** (_Küçük ödül_):
A present owned by one Game. She wins it when her Best score in that Game reaches 95%. It costs
no Points. The slot machine is the exception: its jackpot wins its Small prize.
_Avoid_: Bonus, gift (a Gift is bought)

**Return** (_İade_):
She gives back a Gift she holds. Half of its price comes back to her Balance.
_Avoid_: Refund, cancel

**Proof** (_Belge_):
The printable page that lists the Gifts she holds and the Small prizes she won. Atıl hands over
what the Proof lists.
_Avoid_: Receipt, certificate

**Save code** (_Yedek kodu_):
A line of text that holds all her progress. She copies it to back up, pastes it to restore or to
move to another device, and the Proof prints it. Its last characters are a check code that
catches a typo; it does not stop an edit made on purpose.
_Avoid_: Password, export, token
