# Birthday games

A hidden games site at `/20261209/`, a birthday gift for Çağla. She plays short games, earns
Points, and spends them on Gifts. All text she sees is in Turkish. Charted by a wayfinder map
labelled `wayfinder:map`.

## Language

**Game** (_Oyun_):
One playable activity on the site. A Game gives a score from 0% to 100% on each Round.
_Avoid_: Level, challenge

**Round** (_Tur_):
One try at a Game, from its start to its result. Each Round gives one score. One spin of the
slot machine is one Round.
_Avoid_: Play, game, try, turn

**Best score** (_Rekor_):
The highest score she ever reached in one Game. Only the Best score counts; other Rounds leave
no trace in her Points.
_Avoid_: High score, total score

**Points** (_Puan_):
The currency of the site. She earns Points from her Best scores in the skill Games and spends
them on Gifts. The slot machine and the Prize Games give none, so 1,000 is the most she can earn.
_Avoid_: Credits, coins, score (a score belongs to one Round)

**Balance** (_Bakiye_):
The Points she can spend now: Points earned plus Gifted points, minus the price she paid for every
Gift she ever bought, plus half of that price for every Gift she Returned.
_Avoid_: Wallet, total

**Gift** (_Hediye_):
A present she buys with Points. Its price leaves her Balance at once.
_Avoid_: Reward, unlock, prize (a prize is not bought)

**Gifted points** (_Hediye puan_):
Points Atıl gives her by hand, outside any Game. They add to her Balance but are not Points
earned, so the 1,000 that the Games can give stays the most she can earn. The Proof lists them.
_Avoid_: Bonus, gift (a Gift is bought), earned points

**Small prize** (_Küçük ödül_):
A present owned by one Game. She wins it when her Best score in that Game reaches 95%, in a skill
Game and in a Prize Game alike. It costs no Points. The slot machine is the exception: its jackpot
wins its Small prize.
_Avoid_: Bonus, gift (a Gift is bought)

**Prize Game** (_Ödül oyunu_):
A Game that gives no Points: her Best score in it counts only for its Small prize, won at 95% as
in a skill Game. Its prize is worth more than most, so the Game is hard on purpose. The slot
machine is not one: it is the one luck Game.
_Avoid_: Bonus game, bonus

**Return** (_İade_):
She gives back a Gift she holds. Half of its price comes back to her Balance.
_Avoid_: Refund, cancel

**Proof** (_Belge_):
The printable page that lists the Gifts she holds and the Small prizes she won. Atıl hands over
what the Proof lists.
_Avoid_: Receipt, certificate

**Subject point** (_Odak noktası_):
The one point Atıl marks on a photo in the photo framing Game: the thing the photo is about. Her
crop scores by how close it puts this point to a rule-of-thirds point.
_Avoid_: Focus, center, target

**Save code** (_Yedek kodu_):
A line of text that holds all her progress. She copies it to back up, pastes it to restore or to
move to another device, and the Proof prints it. Its last characters are a check code that
catches a typo; it does not stop an edit made on purpose.
_Avoid_: Password, export, token
