## Notes on the three ambiguities

**Mont-Saint-Martin.** The user confirmed the town on the France–Luxembourg border, not Mont-Saint-Michel. It is a commune in Meurthe-et-Moselle (Grand Est), with about 9,361 people in 2023. It lies against the Belgium–France–Luxembourg tripoint, near Athus (BE) and Rodange (LU), and next to Longwy, with which it forms one urban area ([fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont-Saint-Martin_(Meurthe-et-Moselle))). Its own sights are small: the Saint-Martin church on its butte, an older Romanesque church, the Piedmont viaduct, and the 12 ha cross-border Parc Brigidi. Community trail sites list forest walks and MTB loops from the town into the Belgian Gaume ([randozone](https://www.randozone.com/v/randonnee-mont-saint-martin-54350)). Next door, Longwy's fortified town is part of the UNESCO *Fortifications of Vauban* listing ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1283)). **Implication:** as a 1-week Gift it works as a cheap base for hiking in Luxembourg, the Gaume and Lorraine. That overlaps with the "Luxembourg hiking" weekend Gift. It is about 250 km from Eindhoven (est.). I found no official tourist-office source with lodging prices.

**VinylFarm.EU.** Its own site calls it "a large vinyl record warehouse, Discogs seller and creative home for record stories, Vinyl Sessions, digging events and vinyl culture" ([vinylfarm.eu](https://vinylfarm.eu/)). The warehouse is in **Oirschot, Netherlands**, and "the exact address will be shared after you sign up" ([digging event page](https://vinylfarm.eu/digging)). Ways to visit: digging events (the last one was 29 Nov 2025, capped at 20 diggers, €2–3.50 per record; no 2027 date was published), a warehouse laser game (Duo €15, Squad of 4 €25, no Sundays, booked by Instagram DM; [page](https://vinylfarm.eu/lasergame-at-vinylfarm)), and free one-hour DJ "Vinyl Sessions". Day to day it sells through Discogs. **Implication:** "visit VinylFarmEU" needs an event or an arranged slot. It is not a drop-in.

**Urban Shopper.** This is a small indoor shopping centre on the ground floor of the former Philips **Anton** building on Strijp-S (Torenallee 60), with 18 independent shops in wooden units: design, vintage, fashion, lighting and gifts ([urbanshopper.nl](https://www.urbanshopper.nl)). For a **clock**, the matching shop is **De Tijdmeter**, a watch and clock maker that also repairs. For "a gift of her choice", the site names **Timeless** for gifts and **De Zitfabriek** for design furniture and home accessories. It is open Wed–Sun 11:00–18:00, and daily during Dutch Design Week, per a secondary listing ([Stayokay](https://www.stayokay.com/en/tips/urban-shopper-in-eindhoven)).
